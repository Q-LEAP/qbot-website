How it works

How does Q-BOT automate your 2FA?

A fully automated process, in under 10 seconds.

1

Trigger

Your test script calls the Q-BOT API. The actuator presses the button on the 2FA device (LuxTrust, smartphone, etc.).

2

Capture & analysis

The HD camera captures the device's illuminated screen. Computer vision extracts the OTP code in real time.

3

API return

The OTP code is sent back to your script in under 10 seconds. It is automatically entered into your application.

4

Security

The photo is deleted immediately after processing. No data is retained outside Q-BOT.

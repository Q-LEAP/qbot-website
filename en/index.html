Technical Specs
In five steps

How it works

Q-Bot takes over the moment your automated test reaches the two-factor authentication step.

1

Your test reaches the 2FA step

The automated journey hits the step that needs an approval on a smartphone.

2

Q-Bot is triggered

Your test calls Q-Bot, or Q-Bot reacts on its own to an incoming notification.

3

Q-Bot drives the smartphone

It runs the saved scenario directly in the genuine 2FA app.

4

The approval goes through

The request is approved, or the required code is retrieved.

5

Your test carries on

The automated journey resumes with no human intervention.

See how it works

Build an automation visually, without writing code

Scenarios are built in the web interface, on top of screenshots of the app under test. No scripting, no selectors to maintain.

  • You drop in a screenshot, which becomes the background of the step
  • You place the tap points by clicking, and set each wait time
  • You add, reorder or remove a step at any time
  • The scenario is saved on the device, and reusable at will
Benefits

What Q-Bot changes for a test team

It removes the one step test tools cannot get past, without bypassing it: the approval happens in the genuine 2FA app, on a genuine smartphone.

No human intervention

No tester needed to approve the 2FA step. The run goes through, at any hour.

On the genuine 2FA app

Deterministic execution: Q-Bot replays predefined taps in the genuine app, with no visual recognition of the interface during execution.

Simple to integrate

One HTTP call from your test pipeline, or the companion app reacting to the notification. No SDK, no agent to install.

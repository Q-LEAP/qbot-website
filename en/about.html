About

A product born in the field

Q-Bot is a Q-Leap solution, built to automate what conventional testing tools still cannot.

Q-Bot came out of a concrete problem

Our own teams kept hitting the same wall: a test scenario could be automated end to end, right up to the point where two-factor authentication called for an action on a real smartphone.

That one step meant a human had to step in, and the whole automation chain stopped there.

Q-Bot was built to remove that last manual step, so that end-to-end tests actually run all the way through.

Craft

Designed and developed in Luxembourg

Q-Bot is designed, developed and assembled in Luxembourg by the Q-Leap teams.

From the software design to the hardware integration, every step is kept in-house, for a solution that answers what automated testing demands.

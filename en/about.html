About

Q-Bot, built by software testing experts

Q-Bot is developed by Q-Leap, a Luxembourg company specialised in software testing and quality assurance since 2012.

Where it comes from

Q-Bot came out of a concrete need

For several years, Q-Leap has been building ways to automate more of software testing and to cut down manual steps.

Q-Bot came out of that work, in our Bertrange offices: automating the one step that remained hard to fit into end-to-end tests, two-factor authentication on a smartphone.

The product was developed under the lead of Sylvain Perez, founder and CEO of Q-Leap.

Origin

Designed and developed in Luxembourg

Q-Bot is designed, developed and assembled by Q-Leap in Luxembourg.

Its design draws directly on our experience of test environments and of the automation problems our clients run into.

How it works

How does Q-BOT work?

A 5-step fully automated process, with no human intervention.

1. Trigger

The actuator presses the button on the LuxTrust device or mobile application, triggering the OTP code display.

2. Illumination

High-intensity LEDs illuminate the device screen to ensure optimal image capture.

3. Capture

The high-resolution camera captures the OLED screen image showing the OTP code.

4. Processing

Computer vision analyzes the image and extracts the OTP code. Photos are deleted immediately after processing.

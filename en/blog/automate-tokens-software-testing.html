Q-Leap is officially launching its Q-Bot robot! This is a unique solution on the market for automating software tests that require two-factor authentication via a LuxTrust token. This robot automatically retrieves the value of the OTP (One Time Password) and unlocks the systems or applications that require two-factor authentication (2FA).

Q-Bot, the solution for automating tests with LuxTrust tokens.

Testing experts, in particular those in charge of automating end-to-end test processes, must be able to work without any barrier. Skipping certain features such as two-factor authentication puts the quality of the delivered application at risk. With Q-Bot, these features can now be tested in full, which results in a higher quality of the delivered application.

« Joao, one of our colleagues, came to see me. He was on assignment with one of our clients, in a team in charge of testing a banking application. He told me about a problem that is well known in the testing world: the impossibility of testing an application end to end when it requires two-factor authentication with security tokens. After a few discussions, we decided to design a robot to solve it. »

This small enclosure, barely 10 cm on each side, was born in the summer of 2022. It was developed in-house at the offices of Q-Leap, a company specialised in software testing. Already in place in several companies in Luxembourg, this solution automates test cases, even the most critical ones, which require validation with a token.

Two-factor authentication, beneficial and secure, is a constraint in functional test cases. « With Q-Bot, it now becomes possible to automate 100% of feature tests », says S. Perez.

Q-Bot's features

Q-Bot works with any type of Web, Mobile or Desktop application. The use cases are numerous: authenticating a user, creating a user account, confirming an e-commerce transaction, resetting a user's password, validating a money transfer, signing a document, requesting an official document…

This robot therefore supports many development, testing, integration and business process automation projects (Robot Process Automation, or RPA) that require validation with a token.

A simple, safe and effective solution!

  • Used through its two types of access: Web and API
  • No software installation required. A simple connection to the company network is all that is needed.
  • Retrieving the token value takes less than 10 seconds.
  • Compatible with Selenium, Katalon, Robot Framework and all other automation solutions thanks to its simple, secure API.

The first version of Q-Bot was designed specifically to work with tokens from the LuxTrust brand, leader on the authentication market in Luxembourg. In its second version, coming soon, Q-Bot will also support all « physical » tokens on the market as well as smartcards. Finally, a version dedicated to two-factor authentication on smartphones is planned.

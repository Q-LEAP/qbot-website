How it works

How does Q-Bot automate your 2FA?

Your test calls the Q-Bot API, or the companion app detects the 2FA notification on the phone by itself. Q-Bot then replays the saved scenario: taps, swipes and wait times land on the device's physical screen over ADB. The step clears in under ten seconds and the test resumes.

1

Build

Build the scenario in the web interface: upload a screenshot of the 2FA screen, place numbered tap points, set wait times. No scripting required.

2

Trigger

Your test scenario calls the Q-Bot API. Or the companion app fires on its own, the instant a 2FA notification reaches the device.

3

Execute

Q-Bot replays the scenario on the smartphone connected over USB: taps, swipes and waits, via ADB.

4

Continue

2FA handled. Pipeline unblocked. The test continues from exactly where it left off.

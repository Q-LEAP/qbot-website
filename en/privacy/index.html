Privacy policy for the Q-Bot Mobile application

Your privacy is important to us. That’s why we process as little data as possible. That’s why we process as little data as possible.

Q-Bot Mobile app scans the local environment to find and connect to your Q-Bot Device through BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy). Depending on the device, the Q-Bot Mobile app would require location, bluetooth, accessibility and nearby permissions enabled. In addition, Q-Bot Mobile app listens to notifications received by another app the user previously selected and sends the text of the notifications to Q-Bot Device via BLE.

The bluetooth, location and nearby permission enable the Q-Bot app to scan, identify and connect to the Q-Bot device through BLE. The accessibility permission is required for the Q-Bot app to select a particular app whose 2FA test cases need to be automated. The accessibility permission enables the Q-Bot to read the notifications received by the selected app. Please note, the accessibility permission does not enable Q-Bot app to help individuals with accessibility, rather it provides a means for the user to listen to the notification of the other app selected by the user.

Q-Bot Mobile app does not communicate nor exchange data with any other system except with Q-Bot Device. The data sent to Q-Bot Device corresponds to the text received by the app the user selected. The text is used to trigger actions on Q-Bot Device but it is not saved. The Q-Bot app and the device work together to automate 2FA use case scenarios for the QA team.

The user of the Q-Bot mobile app does not need to do any kind of login or registration to use the app. No personally identifiable information is collected from the app.
